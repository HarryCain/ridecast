# Data attribution and license

Source: Fanaee-T, H. (2013). Bike Sharing [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894

The UCI dataset is offered under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Ridecast derives historical features, predictions, summaries, and diagnostics from
the source hourly CSV. The original data is downloaded separately rather than
included here. Derived dataset artifacts in `artifacts/*.csv` and the dataset-derived
contents of `artifacts/summary.json` retain this attribution and CC BY 4.0 licensing.
The project's MIT license applies to source code, not a relicensing of the dataset.
